Privacy Policy

NolimitCoder V2 by NolimitCode • last updated: September 2026

1. What Stays Only on Your PC

  • App conversations, projects, and settings.
  • Files in folders you never show to the app.

None of this is sent anywhere — the app works locally and reaches the cloud only when you send a message.

2. What Is Sent to the NolimitCode Cloud

  • The text of your messages and the selected model (NolimitCoderV2 / V3).
  • Files attached as context via @path/file.
  • Tool outputs needed for the answer (e.g. project search results).

Data is used only to process your request and return the response.

3. What We Do Not Collect

We require no registration or email, we do not track you with advertising cookies, and we do not sell any data to third parties.

4. Your Rights

You can delete your conversation history at any time directly in the app. If you have questions about data processing, contact us (contact to be added).
